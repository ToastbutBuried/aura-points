# How to earn Aura

_244 quests, generated from the live quest book._

Aura is the currency on the `aura_points` scoreboard. Spend it on abilities, rerolls, skill points and cosmetics.

## Built into the mod

These pay Aura on any server (default values, all configurable):

| Source | Aura |
|---|---|
| Weekly challenges (`/aura weekly`, 3 per week) | 40 / 60 / 80 per challenge, +100 for all three |
| Killing an aura-touched mob (glowing, named) | 5 |
| Team combo hitting its first enemy (outside the arena) | 5 for both players (with a cooldown) |
| Winning a duel (`/aura arena`) | 50, plus the wager pot |
| Winning an arena match | 30 |
| Wave Survival | 2 per wave cleared, +10 per boss wave |
| Killing a boss (`#aurapoints:bosses`) | 0 by default (`BOSS_AURA_PER_KILL`); unlocks the PvP skill tree |

## Quests

Everything below is the quest book of our server (FTB Quests + a small reward datapack). It is not shipped with the mod, but shows how a server can pay Aura. Repeatable quests roll a chance on every claim; one-time quests always pay.

| Tier | Repeatable | One-time |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 10% chance of 5 Aura | 5 Aura |
| Normal | 25% chance of 10 Aura | 10 Aura |
| Hard | 50% chance of 20 Aura | 20 Aura |
| Very Hard | 75% chance of 35 Aura | 35 Aura |
| Rare | 50 Aura | 50 Aura |
