# Resonance (retired)

Resonance is **out of the rolling pool and out of all further updates**. Players who already hold it keep it; nobody can roll it any more. Existing content stays as it was. It has no Ultimate, no third cast and no relic.

**Role:** sound hunter. You are nearly blind, but you hear everything.

| | |
|---|---|
| Keys | Ability 1: **Abyssal Grasp**, 2: **Listening Tree**, 3: **Sonic Blossom** |
| Passive | Echolocation |
| Branches (Mastery V) | **Dirge** (ranged hunter) or **Chorus** (plant zone control) |

- **Abyssal Grasp** *(hold)*: wind four tentacles back; the longer you hold, the further they reach. Let go and they seize the first mob or player and whip them to you. You hold them in front of you (they can fight but not move) until you press again and throw them; thrown into a wall they take more damage.
- **Listening Tree**: plant a tree with a curled tip. It grabs the loudest target near it and leashes them to itself for a while; every step away is harder. A few hits from someone else break it.
- **Sonic Blossom**: a hollow blossom grows under you and listens. Anyone sprinting or louder nearby gets a dart every moment, and whoever it hits glows for you, even sneaking.
- **Echolocation** *(passive)*: a black-violet haze swallows everything past a short distance, but every player and mob nearby that makes a sound glows for you, even through walls. Only sneaking or standing still hides them.

Chains: **Echo Crush** (Sonic Blossom, then Abyssal Grasp) and **Attuned** (Listening Tree, then Sonic Blossom).
