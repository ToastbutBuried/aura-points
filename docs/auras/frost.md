# Frost

> *"Frost is patience made into a weapon."*
> *Its pillar was cut from a glacier that took ten thousand winters to reach the sea.*
> *What it holds, it holds still. Even time, for a breath.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore frost`)

**Role:** control. One sharp tool, a shell of ice and a long memory for cold.

## Abilities
- **Ice Spike** *(hold)*: form a spike of ice and let go to throw it. It slows what it hits; **fully charged** it freezes the target solid. A hit on a frozen target breaks them free with a short burst of speed. A hit gives you Speed to catch up.
- **Sure-Footed** *(passive)*: ice is never slippery for you.
- **Ice Shell** *(passive)*: a shell of ice layers soaks a share of every hit, layer by layer. Once you are left alone, cracks mend and a broken layer grows back (faster somewhere cold). Every hit you land on a player speeds it up.
- **Shatter** *(passive)*: land several charged hits in a row, quickly, with no misses and no item switching, and the last one shatters for extra damage.

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Black Ice**: Frost Path, then an Ice Spike: the spike hits harder and chills.
- **Cold Snap**: an Ice Spike, then Frost Path: part of the cooldown is refunded.

## Reactions
Frost leaves **Chilled**. A Frost hit on a Wet target sets off *Freeze*; a Fire hit on a Chilled target sets off *Melt*. Glacier is the [team combo](../features/team-combos.md) of an Abyssal Bubble and an Ice Spike.

## Ultimate: Permafrost
A freezing field spreads around you, ice spikes erupt and every enemy freezes solid, then it all shatters.

## Branches
- **Glacial** *(defensive lockdown)*: longer freezes, a longer slow, more Ice Shell layers. Price: a slower shell rebuild and more hits for Shatter.
- **Frostbite** *(burst around the combo)*: Shatter needs fewer hits and **Brittle** makes Chilled targets take more damage from you. Price: fewer shell layers and shorter freezes.
