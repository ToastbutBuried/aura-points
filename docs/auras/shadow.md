# Shadow

> *"Before the first dawn there was only the dark, and it was not empty."*
> *Shadow is what learned to move in it unseen. Its pillar was raised in a night that never lifted.*
> *Those who wake it learn the dark was never against them.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore shadow`)

**Role:** fast, stealthy skirmisher. Strike unseen, vanish, be somewhere else.

| | |
|---|---|
| Keys | Ability 1: **Dash**, Ability 2: **Vanish** (Ability 3 is empty) |
| Passives | Phantom Step, Vanish Step, Bloodrush, Nightstalker (+ Vein of Aura, which every aura has) |
| Ultimate | **Eclipse** (Dark branch: **Singularity**) |
| Branches (Mastery V) | **Phantom** or **Dark** |
| Handheld | Soul Reaper (Umbral Ambush) |
| Strong against | nobody |
| Weak against | Light (but not the Dark branch) |
| Reaction mark | none |

## Abilities
- **Dash**: burst forward.
- **Vanish**: turn completely invisible, armor too, with a speed boost, and leave a black trail on the floor.
- **Phantom Step** *(passive)*: sneak while falling and you take no fall damage and vanish for a moment on landing.
- **Vanish Step** *(passive)*: land a Phantom Step while vanished and you stay vanished longer. There is no cooldown, so every landing adds more.
- **Bloodrush** *(passive)*: Speed and Haste after every player kill.
- **Nightstalker** *(passive)*: the darker it is, the harder you hit. Full darkness is the maximum, light takes it away. It also gives you night vision (`/aura nightvision` switches it).

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Dash | **Backstep**: a short dash backwards, invisible for a moment | **Phase Dash**: pass through enemies, hurting and blinding them |
| Vanish | **Decoy**: vanish and leave a shadow clone where you stood | **Deep Vanish**: lasts longer, but no speed |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Shade Step**: Vanish, then Dash: the Dash hits harder and you get speed.
- **Slip Away**: Dash, then Vanish: part of Vanish's cooldown is refunded.

## Ultimate: Eclipse
Darkness falls on every enemy player in range. You vanish and blink behind several enemies in a row, striking each one.

## Branches
- **Phantom** *(evasion and tricks)*: shorter Vanish and Dash cooldowns, Dash leaves a shadow clone that makes hunting mobs lose you, longer Phantom Step and Vanish Step.
- **Dark** *(pull, rift and null)*: the old Void aura. A full kit swap: Dash and Vanish are given up for three Void actives and two Void passives, and Singularity replaces Eclipse.

### Dark kit
| | |
|---|---|
| **Void Pull** | open the void in your palm and drag everything in front of you to you |
| **Rift Step** | step through a tear in space; the rift stays open behind you and hurts whoever touches it. *Sneak cast:* **Rift Anchor** marks where you stand and a second sneak press steps you back to it |
| **Null Orb** | a slow orb of nothing: a player it hits cannot use their aura for a while, mobs are weakened |
| **Event Horizon** *(passive)* | projectiles that come close to you slow to a crawl |
| **Entropy** *(passive)* | everything you hit loses armor for a few seconds |
| **Singularity** *(ultimate)* | a black hole opens where you aim, drags in every enemy and grinds them, then collapses |

Dark chains: **Collapse** (Void Pull, then Null Orb) and **Rift Snare** (Rift Step, then Void Pull).
Dark has matchups of its own: it deals more damage to Light and Wind auras, and Light's advantage over Shadow does not reach it.
