**Role:** duelist. Trade your own health for power and take it back from the enemy.

## The blood bar
Bleeding cuts fill your **blood bar** every second (more for players than monsters, monsters more than animals). Crimson Sacrifice can spend some of it to cost you less health, and the Blood Moon ultimate needs it full enough. Dying empties it.

## Abilities
- **Hemorrhage**: lunge where you look and cut the first mob or player you reach. They bleed for a few seconds.
- **Blood Tether**: bind the target in your sights with a thread of blood. Every moment it drains health from them into you. It snaps if they get too far or out of sight.
- **Crimson Sacrifice**: give some of your own health: your next few melee hits deal multiplied damage. The blood bar can pay part of the price.
- **Bloodlust** *(passive)*: every kill heals you.
- **Thin Blood** *(passive)*: below a health threshold you run faster.

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Hemorrhage | **Transfusion**: every bleeding enemy nearby stops bleeding, takes damage and heals you | **Deep Cut**: more damage, longer bleed |
| Blood Tether | **Lifeline**: the ally (or your pet) in your sights gets Regeneration and Resistance | **Leech**: drains more, heals you a share |
| Crimson Sacrifice | **Pinprick**: a cheap, small version | none |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Blood Price**: Crimson Sacrifice, then Hemorrhage: a much harder cut that heals you.
- **Open Vein**: Hemorrhage, then Blood Tether: a stronger tether that weakens.

## Ultimate: Blood Moon
Needs a full enough blood bar. The sky turns red for everyone near you: every hit you land heals you and every enemy nearby bleeds.

## Branches
- **Sanguine** *(aggressive sustain)*: Hemorrhage and every bleed tick heal you, Bloodlust heals more, the bar fills faster. Price: a weaker Sacrifice multiplier and a dearer Blood Moon.
- **Sacrificial** *(high risk, high reward)*: Sacrifice costs a flat amount of health, ignores the bar and multiplies harder; the tether drains more but costs you health and no longer heals you.
