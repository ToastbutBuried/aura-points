# Verdant

> *"Everything that grows is a little bit stubborn."*
> *Verdant's pillar had roots before the hall had walls; the floor around it is still cracked.*
> *It feeds, it binds, it poisons - and it always comes back.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore verdant`)

**Role:** trapper and poisoner. Pull them in, root them, let the poison work.

| | |
|---|---|

| Passives | Bloom, Virulence |

## Abilities
- **Vine Hook**: two roots burst from the ground and reach for your crosshair, catch the first mob or player and drag them to you, straight through walls.
- **Entanglement**: a root trap. Root arms rise where you look (or under someone's feet) as a warning, then thrash about waiting. Whoever stays in reach is seized, yanked into the middle and wound up: they cannot move until they break free by mashing W A S D or cutting the green arms. Hits loosen the roots. If nobody is caught, the arms pump out a poison cloud before they sink. One trap at a time.
- **Verdant Viper**: send out a viper of roots toward your crosshair; it bites the first mob or player (damage and Poison). While it is out you cannot move, eat or heal, but nothing can hurt you. Other players can kill it, and then you are stunned and hurt. Press again to call it back.
- **Bloom** *(passive)*: while you sneak, crops, saplings and plants nearby grow as if bone-mealed (`/aura bloom` switches it).
- **Virulence** *(passive)*: poisoned targets take more damage from you. Your Entanglement, bites and Toxic Clouds all poison.

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Vine Hook | **Harvest Hook**: dropped items and animals in front of you are drawn to you | **Long Hook**: more range, less damage |
| Entanglement | **Overgrowth**: crops and saplings around the aimed spot grow | **Thicket**: several traps side by side |
| Verdant Viper | **Venom Spit**: a quick spit at the nearest enemy, you stay free to move | none |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Snare**: Vine Hook, then Entanglement: the trap hits harder and slows.
- **Ambush Bite**: Entanglement, then Verdant Viper: a stronger bite with extra poison.

## Reactions
Verdant leaves **Poisoned**. A Fire hit on a poisoned target sets off *Toxic Cloud*; a Verdant hit on a Wet target sets off *Overgrowth*. Poison clouds feed the Toxic Gale [team combo](../features/team-combos.md).

## Ultimate: Overgrowth
A great tree bursts up behind you. Its roots hold every enemy nearby (thorns hurt them) and its petals heal you.

## Branches
- **Venomweaver** *(damage over time)*: a stronger Virulence, and poison stacks to a higher level on a poisoned target. Price: a longer Viper cooldown.
- **Grove Warden** *(support and trapper)*: friends near you heal while Bloom is on and you sneak, traps last longer and need more key presses to break. Price: no Virulence, and a weaker Viper poison.
