# Water

> *"The deep remembers every shore it ever touched."*
> *Water's pillar stood under a sea that has long since left, and the veins still run like a tide.*
> *It heals as easily as it drowns. The awakened decide which.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore water`)

**Role:** sustain and control. Keep your friends up, pull enemies in, drown the rest.

| | |
|---|---|
| Keys | Ability 1: **Shockwave**, 2: **Healing Tide**, 3: **Abyssal Bubble** |
| Passives | Iron Skin, Flow |
| Ultimate | **Tidal Ascension** |
| Branches (Mastery V) | **Tidecaller** or **Leviathan** |

| Weak against | Verdant, Thunder |

## Abilities
- **Shockwave**: blast enemies around you away; the closer they are, the harder it hits.
- **Healing Tide**: you and every friendly player nearby get Regeneration, and every negative effect is washed away. In the arena it reaches your teammates.
- **Abyssal Bubble** *(hold)*: a bubble follows you; whoever is inside runs out of air and is slowly dragged toward you. Your camera shakes harder the longer you hold, and if your crosshair drifts too far from where you started the bubble breaks.
- **Iron Skin** *(passive)*: less damage from every source, magic included.
- **Flow** *(passive)*: in water or rain you regenerate, breathe, swim faster, hit harder and recharge faster. Rain gives a smaller boost than water.

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Shockwave | **Undertow**: pulls enemies in and soaks them (Wet) | **Tidal Slam**: more range, damage and knockback |
| Healing Tide | **Mending Tide**: allies only, one Regeneration level higher | **Spring**: also leaves a healing pool at your feet |
| Abyssal Bubble | **Diver's Bubble**: Water Breathing and Slow Falling for you | none (the key already holds) |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Undertow**: Shockwave, then Abyssal Bubble: the bubble hits harder and soaks enemies.
- **Riptide**: Healing Tide, then Shockwave: a harder Shockwave that also heals you.

## Reactions
Water leaves a **Wet** mark. A Frost hit on a wet target sets off *Freeze*, a Thunder hit sets off *Conduct*, a Verdant hit sets off *Overgrowth*. See [reactions](../features/reactions.md).
Water is also half of three [team combos](../features/team-combos.md): Electrified Bubble, Glacier and Boiling Bubble.

## Ultimate: Tidal Ascension
A column of water carries you high up, then you crash down and a ring wave rolls out: damage, everyone swept away, and you heal.

## Branches
- **Tidecaller** *(support)*: a stronger, wider Healing Tide that soaks every enemy in range Wet, more Shockwave knockback. Price: weaker Iron Skin and a longer Bubble cooldown.
- **Leviathan** *(tank)*: every hit you take adds to Iron Skin, stacking up and fading after a few seconds without a hit; the Bubble drags harder. Price: a weaker Healing Tide and Shockwave.
