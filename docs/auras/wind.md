# Wind

> *"Wind has no home, so it built one out of the space between things."*
> *Its pillar hums when no one is near, a note too high to hear for long.*
> *The awakened stop falling. They only ever land.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore wind`)

**Role:** air mobility. Be above, behind, and gone.

| | |
|---|---|
| Keys | Ability 1: **Dash**, 2: **Super Wind Charge** (Double Jump is the jump key) |
| Passives | Gale Rider, Featherfall |
| Ultimate | **Tempest** |
| Branches (Mastery V) | **Skyrunner** or **Cyclone** |
| Handheld | Stormcaller Glaive (Eye of the Storm) |
| Strong against | nobody |
| Weak against | the Dark branch of Shadow |
| Reaction mark | none (but Wind sets off *Firestorm* and *Storm*) |

## Abilities
- **Dash**: burst forward. Wind's Dash has more to it than anyone else's:
  - dash up and press it again high in the air to **Dive**: no fall damage and a shockwave where you land, stronger the higher you dove from;
  - **hold** the key to blast yourself high into the air and shove everything around you away (on its own cooldown);
  - with an elytra it is a boost forward instead, and holding it gives a big one.
- **Super Wind Charge**: fire a wind charge with many times the power, no wind charges needed. **Air Bending**: hold the key instead to gather a ball of wind, swing the camera to charge it and press again: everything near where it hits is hurled far away and high up.
- **Double Jump**: press jump again in mid-air. With an elytra it lifts you a little instead.
- **Gale Rider** *(passive)*: more damage while gliding with an elytra.
- **Featherfall** *(passive)*: less fall damage; sneak in mid-air to drift down slowly.

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Dash | **Tailwind**: Speed for a while, short cooldown | none (the key already holds) |
| Super Wind Charge | **Gust**: a push on yourself (or the boat you ride) along your look, no fall damage | none |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Slipstream**: Dash, then Super Wind Charge: the charge launches enemies up.
- **Tailspin**: Super Wind Charge, then Dash: Dash gets a big cooldown refund.

## Reactions
Wind leaves no mark, but it fires reactions: a Wind hit on a Burning target is *Firestorm*, on a Charged one *Storm*. Wind bursts also trigger the Fire Tornado and Toxic Gale [team combos](../features/team-combos.md).

## Ultimate: Tempest
You rise into the eye of a tornado. It follows you, sucks in every enemy nearby, whirls them around and flings them all away.

## Branches
- **Skyrunner** *(mobility)*: Dash and Double Jump get two charges, Gale Rider's bonus lingers after you land. Price: a longer Super Wind Charge cooldown and a smaller Air Bending blast.
- **Cyclone** *(crowd control)*: the Air Bending blast pulls enemies in instead of throwing them out; Firestorm and Storm reactions hit harder. Price: longer Dash and Double Jump cooldowns.
