# Fire

> *"The first flame was stolen, they say, and it has burned angry ever since."*
> *Fire's pillar is the warmest stone in the hall; keepers once cooked on it.*
> *Waking it is easy. Keeping it from waking you is the hard part.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore fire`)

**Role:** attack. Burn things, finish what is hurt.

## Abilities
- **Fireball**: a fireball that damages, sets its target ablaze and starts a few fires where it hits.
- **Heat Wave**: set everything around you on fire and blast it back. Use it while holding a fully charged Fire Bending flame for the **Inferno Slam**: a far bigger blast and a much longer burn.
- **Fire Bending**: hold a ball of fire in front of you. Swing the camera around to charge it, then press again to fire it at your crosshair: strong up close, weaker the farther it flies. Fully charged it shows you every mob and player in front of you, even through a few blocks of wall.
- **Berserker's Aura** *(passive)*: the lower your health, the more damage you deal.
- **Executioner** *(passive)*: bonus damage to targets below a health threshold.
- **Fireborn** *(passive)*: fire cannot hurt you (burning, campfires, magma blocks, fireballs). Only lava still burns, but you see through it.

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Fireball | **Spread Shot**: several small fireballs in a fan | **Meteor**: a slow, heavy fireball with a big blast |
| Heat Wave | **Flame Ward**: a ring of fire around you, no knockback | **Inferno Wave**: more range and damage, burns longer |
| Fire Bending | **Flame Cloak** (with no flame in hand): Fire Resistance, enemies nearby catch fire, dropped items next to you are smelted | none |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Stoked**: Fire Bending, then Fireball: the Fireball hits harder and ignites.
- **Backdraft**: Fireball, then Heat Wave: a harder Heat Wave.

## Reactions
Fire leaves **Burning**. Fire hitting a Poisoned target sets off *Toxic Cloud*, hitting a Chilled one *Melt*; a Water hit on a burning target makes *Steam*, a Wind hit makes *Firestorm*. See [reactions](../features/reactions.md).
Fire zones are part of the Fire Tornado, Magma Field and Boiling Bubble [team combos](../features/team-combos.md).

## Ultimate: Phoenix
Wreathed in flame you fly where you look, burning whoever you pass, then dive down in a blast. Sneak to dive early.

## Branches
- **Blue Flame** *(precision)*: a faster Fireball that pierces, **Scorch** (burning targets take more damage from you), stronger Fire Bending shots. Price: a longer Heat Wave cooldown.
- **Magma** *(zone control)*: Fireball hits, Heat Wave and the Inferno Slam leave magma pools that burn enemies standing in them and give you Resistance. The pools are heat and fire only, no lava blocks. Price: a longer Fireball cooldown.
