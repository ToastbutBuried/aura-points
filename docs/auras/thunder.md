# Thunder

> *"Thunder is the sky losing its temper."*
> *Its pillar was struck a hundred times on the night it was raised and never fell.*
> *The awakened move first and explain later.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore thunder`)

**Role:** burst. Big, fast, paralysing hits.

| | |
|---|---|
| Keys | Ability 1: **Thunderbolt of Zeus**, 2: **Chidori** |
| Passives | Static Charge, Stormborn |
| Ultimate | **Judgement** |
| Branches (Mastery V) | **Surge** or **Conductor** |
| Handheld | Zeus Gauntlets (Chain Strike), plus the **Mjölnir** hammer |
| Strong against | Water |
| Weak against | Verdant, Earth |
| Reaction mark | **Charged** |

## Abilities
- **Thunderbolt of Zeus**: three throws per use. Press: you leap and hang in the air and hurl a bolt at your crosshair (arcing on to nearby enemies). Press again: a quick bolt. Press a third time: **Wrath of Zeus**, a storm gathers where you aim and you call down real lightning, throwing and paralyzing everything near it. Every bolt leaves its target **Charged**, and Wrath hits harder for each charge. You cannot move while you hang.
- **Chidori**: hold the key and lightning gathers in your hand. Let go and you lunge where you look, running the first mob or player through, paralyzed and Charged twice. Let go too early and it fizzles.
- **Static Charge** *(passive)*: every few charged melee hits discharge static that arcs on to enemies nearby and leaves them Charged.
- **Stormborn** *(passive)*: lightning cannot hurt you. In a thunderstorm your lightning hits harder, Thunderbolt recharges faster and Wrath calls down extra bolts.

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Thunderbolt | **Static Rush**: Speed for a while, and it drives the minecart or boat you ride | **Chain Lightning**: a bolt from your hand that jumps from enemy to enemy, no leap |
| Chidori | **Lightning Step**: a blink along your look | none (the key already holds) |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Overcharge**: Thunderbolt, then Chidori: the lunge hits harder and charges the target.
- **Arc Return**: Chidori, then Thunderbolt: part of the cooldown is refunded.

## Reactions
Thunder leaves **Charged**. A Thunder hit on a Wet target sets off *Conduct*; a Wind hit on a Charged target sets off *Storm*. Thunder is the trigger of the Electrified Bubble [team combo](../features/team-combos.md).

## Ultimate: Judgement
You rise into the air and call down a rain of lightning bolts on enemies nearby, then one giant bolt where you aim.

## Branches
- **Surge** *(high tempo)*: a shorter Thunderbolt cooldown, faster Chidori charge, Static Charge fires sooner. Price: a weaker Wrath and a longer Chidori cooldown.
- **Conductor** *(area)*: arcs and chains reach more targets and further; a Charged target you hurt spreads part of the damage to enemies near it. Price: Chidori has one charge and Thunderbolt recharges slower.

## Mjölnir
A heavy hammer: hold the Swing key to whirl it, a full spin draws lightning into it, throw it and steer it in flight. Worthy players only: see [handhelds](../features/handhelds.md).
