# Earth

> *"Under every island here is older stone, and it is listening."*
> *Earth's pillar is the only one the hall was built around rather than beside.*
> *It asks for nothing but weight. Give it yours.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore earth`)

**Role:** tank and terrain. Hard to move, hard to hit.

| | |
|---|---|
| Keys | Ability 1: **Pillar Lift**, 2: **Boulder Toss**, 3: **Quake** |
| Passives | Stone Skin, Grounded |
| Ultimate | **Tectonic Rift** |
| Branches (Mastery V) | **Bulwark** or **Tectonic** |
| Handheld | none |
| Strong against | Thunder |
| Weak against | nobody |
| Reaction mark | none |

## Abilities
- **Pillar Lift**: a stone pillar bursts out under whoever you aim at and throws them high. Aim at nobody and it launches you instead, with no fall damage.
- **Boulder Toss**: tear a boulder out of the ground; it hangs over your head (you walk slower) until you press again, then hurl it where you look: damage and heavy knockback to the first target, splash around it.
- **Quake**: slam the ground and a ripple of heaving earth rolls outward: damage, enemies thrown up and slowed.
- **Stone Skin** *(passive)*: while you stand on stone, dirt, sand or gravel you get extra armor and armor toughness.
- **Grounded** *(passive)*: knockback resistance and less fall damage.

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Pillar Lift | **Catapult**: a pillar under yourself that throws you | **Titan Pillar**: a much taller pillar that stands a while |
| Boulder Toss | **Rubble Spray**: several small rocks in a fan, no pick-up | **Great Boulder**: a bigger boulder, hold to pick it up and press again to throw |
| Quake | **Tunnel**: digs a 3x3 passage through plain stone, dirt, sand and gravel (not in the arena) | **Great Quake**: more range and damage |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Skyfall**: Pillar Lift, then Boulder Toss: the boulder hits harder and slows.
- **Aftershock**: Boulder Toss, then Quake: the quake throws enemies up.

## Reactions and combos
Earth sets off no reactions of its own, but its Quake ripple is half of the Magma Field [team combo](../features/team-combos.md).

## Ultimate: Tectonic Rift
You stamp and the ground splits open ahead of you. Jagged pillars erupt all along the crack and launch everyone on it, then crumble away.

## Branches
- **Bulwark** *(tank)*: far more armor and toughness, more knockback resistance, and every enemy your Quake hits gives you absorption hearts. Price: Pillar Lift and Boulder Toss deal less.
- **Tectonic** *(offense and terrain)*: harder-hitting Pillar Lift, Boulder Toss and a wider Quake. Price: much less Stone Skin and Grounded.
