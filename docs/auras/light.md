# Light

> *"Light does not chase the dark away. It just refuses to leave."*
> *Its pillar was the last raised, to see the others by.*
> *Those who wake it carry a little dawn with them.*
>
> The keeper of the Awakening Altar (`/aura altar lore light`)

**Role:** healer and blinder. Keep people alive, take the enemy's sight.

| | |
|---|---|
| Keys | Ability 1: **Radiant Pulse**, 2: **Solar Flash**, 3: **Prism Beam** |
| Passives | Halo, Luminous |
| Ultimate | **Sunburst** |
| Branches (Mastery V) | **Radiant** or **Prismatic** |

| Strong against | Shadow (not its Dark branch) |
| Weak against | nobody |

## Abilities
- **Radiant Pulse**: a wave of warm light rolls out: you and the players in it heal and regenerate (in the Aura Arena only you), and the undead in it burn.
- **Solar Flash**: a blinding flash from your palm. Everyone in front of you who can see it is blinded and glows, and monsters lose sight of you.
- **Prism Beam** *(hold)*: a beam of pure light streams from your hand to your crosshair, burning the first mob or player in it, more on the undead. You walk slower while you hold it.
- **Halo** *(passive)*: out in the sunlight you heal now and then.
- **Luminous** *(passive)*: you see in the dark, and every few seconds players and monsters nearby light up for a moment, even behind walls.

## Extra casts ([how casts work](../features/casts.md))
| Ability | Sneak + key (Mastery II) | Hold the key (Mastery III) |
|---|---|---|
| Radiant Pulse | **Guiding Light**: Night Vision for you and friends in reach | **Radiant Burst**: more range, healing and undead damage |
| Solar Flash | **Spotlight**: no damage or blindness, only Glowing | **Nova**: a wider cone, longer blindness, more damage |
| Prism Beam | **Mending Beam**: the ally in your sights is healed and regenerates | none (the key already holds) |

## Chains ([how chains work](../features/chains.md))
- **Focused Light**: Solar Flash, then Prism Beam: a stronger beam that weakens.
- **Afterglow**: Radiant Pulse, then Solar Flash: a stronger flash that heals you.

## Ultimate: Sunburst
A small sun rises over you and burns, spearing lances of light at enemies nearby and warming every player near it back to health. Then it bursts: damage and blindness all around.

## Branches
- **Radiant** *(support)*: stronger Pulse and Sunburst healing; Halo shines on friends nearby (Resistance). Price: a weaker beam and a longer Solar Flash cooldown.
- **Prismatic** *(damage)*: a stronger Prism Beam; Solar Flash marks what it hits and the beam on a marked target chains on to more enemies. Price: weaker Pulse and Halo healing.
