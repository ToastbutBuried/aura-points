# The Awakening Altar and Ultimates

Every aura has an **Ultimate**: a big, rare move on its own key (the Ultimate key, bind it under Options > Controls > Aura Points). It is awakened at the **Awakening Altar**, an island east of the arena lobby (`/aura altar`).

## How to awaken
1. Reach **Mastery V** with the aura.
2. Finish the aura's Awakening quest, which opens its ritual at the altar.
3. Pay **800 Aura** and take part in the ritual, a short cutscene around the aura's pillar.

After it, the Ultimate is yours for that aura for good, with a long cooldown (300 s by default, `ULTIMATE_COOLDOWN_SECONDS`).

## The pillars and the Keeper
The altar hall has one **pillar per aura**, and a Keeper who knows its story. `/aura altar lore <aura>` (or the clickable names the Keeper shows) tells it. The same story is quoted on every [aura page](../auras/overview.md). The hall is alive: your aura's pillar answers you, and each pillar murmurs its element.
