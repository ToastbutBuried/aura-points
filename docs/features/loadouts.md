# Loadouts

Up to three **presets per aura** (`LOADOUT_MAX`). A preset holds the **order of your abilities on the Ability 1/2/3 keys**, which cast (tap or sneak) each ability uses by default, and a relic set. Edit them in the Aura Book's Loadout page or with `/aura loadout`. Switching or editing is blocked in combat, in a duel or match and during the Aura Trial.
