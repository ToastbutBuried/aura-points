# Team combos

One player leaves a **zone** (a fire patch, a bubble, a poison cloud), an **ally's** ability passes through it, and the zone turns into a **combo**.

| Combo | Zone | Ally's ability | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fire Tornado | Fire: Heat Wave embers, Fireball fire, the Fire Bending orb | a Wind burst | drifts with the wind, pulls in, burns, then flings everyone out |
| Electrified Bubble | Abyssal Bubble | Thunderbolt, Wrath of Zeus, Chidori | everyone inside zapped and paralyzed, Charged |
| Toxic Gale | poison cloud | a Wind burst | the cloud rolls on as a wall: poison, nausea, damage |
| Glacier | Abyssal Bubble | Ice Spike | everyone inside frozen, then it shatters |
| Magma Field | Fire zones | Earth's Quake ripple | molten ground: slowness, fire, damage |
| Boiling Bubble | Abyssal Bubble | Fireball, Fire Bending, Heat Wave | scalding steam: damage and blindness |
| Prism Burst | Abyssal Bubble | Prism Beam | rays at the nearest enemies, Glowing |

**Allies** are your FTB Teams party or your side in an arena match. Strangers' abilities do nothing to your zone. A combo only hits what both players could hit. Pulling one off pays a little Aura and Mastery XP (with a cooldown) and counts for weekly challenges and advancements.

Each combo is tunable in `aurapoints-combos.json`. `COMBO_PARTY_PROTECTION` (off by default) makes abilities skip your party members everywhere.
