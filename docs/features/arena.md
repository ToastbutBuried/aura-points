# The Aura Arena

A separate dimension with a lobby, a duel ring, stands with live boards, the [Training Hall](training-hall.md) and the [Awakening Altar](awakening-altar.md). Open it with `/aura arena`; `/aura leave` takes you out of anything and back to where you were. Nothing wears out in the arena: tools, weapons and armor lose no durability, and a hit that would kill is a knockout instead.

## Duels
Two players, a set number of rounds, a 3-2-1 countdown on the pads. A map vote picks one of the ring layouts (**Pillars**, **Shattered**, **Terraces**). The loser of a round spectates until the next; after the last round the player with more rounds wins the prize and the wager pot (a tie adds sudden death). `/aura duel`, `/aura arena duel`.

## Matches
`/aura match` (or the NPC menu) starts a group mode:

| Mode | Players | What |
|---|---|---|
| Team Battle | 4+ | two sides |
| Free-for-All | 3+ | everyone against everyone |
| King of the Hill | 2+ | hold the zone |
| **Wave Survival** | 1-8 | hold the ring against endless waves |

### Wave Survival
Mobs come through four gates; every few waves an **aura-touched champion** with a boss bar appears, and mobs get tougher the further you go. Clearing a wave pays everyone in it. After every boss wave you get a short break to heal, and can **switch to any aura you have had**. Every few waves the party **votes on a twist** (three choices, in chat or on the hotbar) that stays for the rest of the run. The score is the last wave cleared; records go on the boards, and "Wave Breaker" is a title.

Arena messages can be shown as titles or in chat (Mods > Aura Points > Config > Feedback).
