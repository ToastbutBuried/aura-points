# Territory shrines

One **ruined shrine** waits in each region of the overworld. Bring the repair materials to its core to rebuild it, then channel at the core (and pay Aura) to **attune** it to your FTB team and your aura.

An owned shrine gives its team a small damage bonus nearby (more for the shrine's own aura) and Regeneration while it is well powered. Its **power fades by the day** and is topped up with Aura. A rival standing at the core **drains** it; at 0 it turns neutral for anyone to take. Owners can also place one with `/aura shrine`.
