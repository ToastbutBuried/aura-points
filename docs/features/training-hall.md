# The Training Hall

Inside the arena. A safe place with dummies that tip over when you hit them, free cooldowns and these things to do:

- **Control Gallery**: kiosks (click the sign) for the hall's settings, the **30-second DPS challenge** with its record board, the **Drill** (a three-step practice run) and the **Rotation** score.
- **Spar Pit**: the only place in the hall where you may fight other players.
- **Shooting range**: a 30-block range with a raised platform and a moving target.
- **Combo trainer**: a live readout over the dummy you last hit: chains, reactions and casts as they go off and a combo counter. A daily set of **challenge cards** gives you things to try.
- **[Aura Trial](aura-trial.md)**: the checklist for your aura.

Records and weekly challenges tie in: "reach X DPS" and "finish X challenge cards" are weekly challenges ([weekly](weekly-and-touched.md)).
