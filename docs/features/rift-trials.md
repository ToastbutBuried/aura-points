# Aura Rifts and Relic Trials

A **rift gate** (a ring of floating stones, a tinted portal curtain and a beam into the sky) opens near a random player in the overworld. Step in and you are pulled to the **Shattered Reliquary**: a ruined cathedral-fortress in its own dimension, different every run, with an approach and gatehouse, an entry hall with difficulty vote plates (Easy to Hardcore, where you have one life), a nave, a crossing under a broken spire and four colored wings.

## The run
1. **Vote** on the difficulty.
2. Find the **Keystones** hidden in decorated pots (each pot has a beam in its wing's color), take a compass off the pedestal to find them, and rebuild each wing's broken portal arch by the model on the table. Rub the Keystones alight (hold right-click, let go in the green zone) and the portal opens.
3. Beat the wing's **trial**. Each frees a **Relic Shard**:
   - **Hall of Echoes**: a path shown over the floor; repeat it. Wrong steps spawn mobs.
   - **Prism Chamber**: aim beams at receivers; every layout is proven solvable.
   - **Choir of Bells**: repeat the song the bells sing.
   - **Weeping Statues**: lift sigils with hold-and-release checks.
   - **Resonance Dig**: pull the shard out of the ground.
   - **Skyward Rings**: fly through rings in a big sky.
4. Put the shards on the altar's pedestals and forge: the floor breaks and everyone drops into the crypt.
5. Defeat the **Relic Warden** ([creatures](creatures-and-bosses.md)).

A run has a time limit; knocked-out players return at the altar, and if everyone is down the run fails. Rift music speeds up as time runs out.

## Rewards
Aura, Mastery XP, and a **relic** ([relics](relics.md)): always for the first clear of an aura, otherwise a chance per player. Clearing rifts is also a weekly challenge.
