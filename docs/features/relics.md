# Elemental relics

One relic per aura except Resonance: **Ember, Tide, Zephyr, Rime, Thorn, Storm, Bedrock, Halo, Dusk and Vein**. Worn in an accessory slot, a relic gives a small *echo* of **another** aura's passive (a relic of your own aura does nothing). A few different relics count at once.

| Relic | Echo |
|---|---|
| Ember (Fire) | more damage to badly hurt targets |
| Tide (Water) | less damage taken from attacks |
| Zephyr (Wind) | less fall damage |
| Rime (Frost) | more damage to frozen or slowed targets |
| Thorn (Verdant) | more damage to poisoned targets |
| Storm (Thunder) | lightning cannot hurt you |
| Bedrock (Earth) | knockback resistance |
| Halo (Light) | a little healing now and then |
| Dusk (Shadow) | faster in the dark |
| Vein (Blood) | lifesteal on melee |

Find them in vanilla loot chests, buy or reroll them with Aura (`/aura relic`), or win them from [Relic Trials](rift-trials.md). [Loadouts](loadouts.md) can remember a relic set.
