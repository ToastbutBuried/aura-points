# Creatures and bosses

## Relic Warden
The boss of the [Reliquary](rift-trials.md): a GeckoLib-animated guardian. Its fight has phases by health:

1. **Guardian**: combos, a ground slam and an attack matching the aura of the run.
2. **Unbound**: homing fragments, a charge lane, mob waves.
3. **Shattered**: four shard pylons you must break.
4. **Overdrive**: faster three-hit combos and a sweeping core beam.
5. **Last Stand**: it kneels and its core is exposed for a time.

Attacks are color-coded: **yellow** = dodge, **blue** = a parry window in the last moments. At 75 / 50 / 25 % health it takes an **Aspect** (Chime, Garden, Prism, Wrath) with its own mechanic.

## Frost Bear
A boss with color variants. A spike man sits inside it (claws and ice spikes); at some health he is thrown out in a blast; it gets faster and angrier, then tires and **flees**. It never dies: everyone who hurt it gets **Blue Bells**. Admin commands: `/aura bear`, `/aura frostbear`.

## Shadow dragon
Built by datapacks from the `dragon_segment_a/b` items, with a rideable head. See the [developer docs](../reference/developers.md).
