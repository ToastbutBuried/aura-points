# Rolling and switching

- Your **first roll is free**. A reroll costs Aura (`REROLL_COST`, 500 by default) and always lands on an aura you **never had**.
- Once you have had several auras, `/aura switch <aura>` takes you back to one of them for a smaller price (`SWITCH_COST`, 150 by default).
- **Mastery, branch and awakening stay with each aura.** Switching away and back loses nothing.
- Abilities you bought stay yours across rerolls, so Dash bought on Shadow is still yours on Wind.
- When you get a new aura, the [Aura Trial](aura-trial.md) is offered.
- Resonance is retired and cannot be rolled any more.

An aura can also be set by an admin: `/aura setaura`.
