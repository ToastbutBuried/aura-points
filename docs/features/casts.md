# Casts: sneak and charged

Most abilities have more than one way to use them.

- **Tap** the key: the normal cast.
- **Sneak + key** (Mastery II): a second cast, often a utility twist (Frost Path, Tunnel, Backstep, Harvest Hook ...).
- **Hold the key** (Mastery III): a third, charged cast that fires on release (Meteor, Titan Pillar, Great Quake ...).

Abilities whose key already means "hold" (Ice Spike, Chidori, Prism Beam, Abyssal Bubble, Wind's Dash and Super Wind Charge, Abyssal Grasp) have no charged cast. Resonance has none either. Sneak and charged casts often take a different share of the cooldown. Every ability's casts are listed in the Aura Book and on the [aura pages](../auras/overview.md).

Server owners: `CAST_VARIANTS_ENABLED`, `CAST_SNEAK_MASTERY`, `CAST_CHARGED_MASTERY` and every cast's numbers are in the aura configs.

The [Aura Trial](aura-trial.md) lends you every cast so you can try them all in the Training Hall.
