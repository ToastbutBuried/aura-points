# The Aura Trial

A **checklist of everything your aura can do**: every active, every sneak and charged cast, every chain and (once awakened) the Ultimate. You work through it in the Training Hall, in any order, with the **whole kit lent to you** whatever you own or whatever your Mastery is.

- Offered when you roll or switch to an aura; start it any time from the Aura Book or `/aura trial`.
- Leaving the hall pauses it; it ends when you log out, change aura or use `/aura trial stop`.
- Finishing it marks it done. You can retake it whenever you like.

It is the quickest way to see what a new aura really does. [Training Hall →](training-hall.md)
