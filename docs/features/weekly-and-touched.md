# Earning Aura: weekly challenges and aura-touched mobs

## Weekly challenges
Three new challenges every week (`/aura weekly`), each with three tiers and an Aura reward. Types: reach DPS in the Training Hall, win duels or duel rounds, slay aura-touched mobs (of one aura), defeat monsters, set off reactions (of one kind), earn Mastery XP, clear a Wave Survival wave, pull off team combos, pull off ability chains, finish Training Hall cards, clear Aura Rifts. Cosmetics can be earned this way too.

## Aura-touched mobs
A share of natural hostile spawns carry an aura. They glow in its color, are named ("Fire-touched Zombie"), have more health and one small ability of their aura. Killing one pays Aura and Mastery XP. They also fill Wave Survival.

## More ways
The full list, including the quest book chapters, is in [QUESTS.md](../../QUESTS.md). Aura can also come from datapacks and commands.
